About EMU

A citizen-led movement, formally built to last.

"Lasting development begins when citizens are empowered to shape the decisions that affect their lives."

Equality Mission Uganda (EMU) is a Ugandan locally led development organization committed to promoting equality through citizen empowerment. Founded in 2019 as a grassroots movement and formally registered as a Company Limited by Guarantee in 2021, EMU works across governance, education, youth leadership, human rights, digital innovation, health transparency, gender equality and community development.

We work collaboratively with communities, public institutions, civil society organizations, academia, development partners and the private sector — placing citizens at the centre of every decision, and strengthening the collaboration required to sustain it.

As EMU continues its transition toward full NGO registration, the organization remains focused on strengthening its institutional systems, expanding strategic partnerships, and delivering community-driven programmes that outlast any single project.

Vision

A just, inclusive society

A just, inclusive and empowered society where all people enjoy equal opportunities, exercise their rights, and actively participate in shaping sustainable development.

Mission

Equality through empowerment

To advance equality by empowering citizens through education, accountable governance, civic engagement, advocacy and inclusive community development.

Purpose

Citizens who can act

We exist to strengthen the capacity of citizens and communities to influence decisions, demand accountability, protect human rights, and contribute to equitable development.
